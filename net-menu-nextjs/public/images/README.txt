PUT YOUR FOOD PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER.

The menu uses exact names from public/menu.js.
For example: public/images/12.jpg -> img: "images/12.jpg".
The logo net-logo.jpg came from your uploaded HTML.
If the menu says "Photo coming soon", that food image file is missing.

To use your own file, change the img field of that food in public/menu.js.
Example: img: "images/chicken-biryani.jpg"

Keep the same spelling, capital letters, spaces and extension (.jpg/.png).
Files in public/images are served as /images/filename.jpg.
